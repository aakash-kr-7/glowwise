## 1. Research method and limits

1. Used the seven focus candidates K01–K07 from Criterion 2, covering all six Glowwise categories.
2. Inspected live Google results with `hl=en&gl=in`. Saved the linked result headings in displayed order and recorded visible search features. These parameters support an India/English research context; they do not eliminate personalisation or localisation.
3. Obtained a separate **Ubersuggest shampoo SERP snapshot updated 10 September 2026**. Kept it distinct from the live 8 October observations.
4. Examined ranking articles and a ranking collection, plus Nykaa's editorial guide linked from that collection. Counted bounded main-content text rather than entire pages containing navigation, footers and unrelated articles.
5. Used **Semrush public website reports for August 2026** as the common competitor keyword/backlink baseline. Smytten's report was updated 18 September; Nykaa's on 17 September. Retrieval on 8 October does not make these October metrics.
6. Checked primary Google and Semrush methodology resources and independently verified a small external-link sample for each competitor.

**Scope:** This is a focused SERP and competitor study, not an exhaustive keyword export, complete backlink audit or national rank-tracking campaign. Positions below describe the captured browser context. Mobile rankings were not separately measured; the resulting Glowwise experience must nevertheless provide equal functionality and information on mobile and desktop.

The requested Ubersuggest, Ahrefs, Serpstat and CompetitorFlow capabilities were investigated. Ubersuggest supplied the cached SERP; report limits or plan restrictions prevented the other requested competitor datasets. ### What Glowwise can reasonably learn

1. **Earn references to something useful:** A transparent pack-size/value comparison, sourcing method or practical grooming selection resource could give relevant publishers a reason to cite Glowwise. This is a proposed opportunity, not an acquired backlink.
2. **Prefer relevant editorial context:** Assess a prospective link's audience and usefulness before treating a high domain score as sufficient.
3. **Do not imitate structural advantages blindly:** Glowwise has no native app to list and should not create one solely to copy Smytten's example. An independent Wikipedia entry is not an appropriate self-promotion tactic.
4. **Keep the course backlink requirement distinct:** Milestone 2 requires links on instructor-designated domains. Those domains are not yet identified here. This analysis supplies a quality rationale; it does not fulfil that later implementation criterion.

## 5. Content-gap analysis: opportunities supported by the evidence

This is a **manual task and content-format comparison**, not a numerical export of every keyword one domain ranks for and another does not. At the 8 October research date Glowwise was unlaunched; no domain rankings or near-miss positions were measured. “Gap” below means a specific improvement to investigate in the sampled experience, not proven absence across an entire competitor site.

| Query / area | What already exists | Bounded opportunity for Glowwise | Concrete deliverable and verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| **K01 shampoo** | Smytten offers FAQs, product cards, Indian context and trials; Pilgrim and Kérastase offer concern-based collections. | Make differences between eligible products directly comparable. A common comparison grid and per-product price-check dates were not identified in the sampled Smytten primary guide. | One owner guide linked to product records; consistent size, price/date/source, relevant attributes and limitations. Verify every included variant. |
| **K02 sunscreen** | Deyga already provides a ten-product table, prices, pack sizes and texture discussion; other results include brands and a publisher. | Provide a documented selection method and source links per factual attribute. The sampled Deyga conclusion favours its own product; Glowwise can explain its commercial relationships and selection basis openly. | A sourced shortlist and comparison with checked labels, dates and explicitly unverified fields. No universal suitability or borrowed expert endorsement. |
| **K03 perfume budget** | Ajmal and other sites have price collections; MensXP has a five-product list with pros/cons; Nykaa has fragrance-finder budget taxonomy. | Make the exact purchasable variant and budget eligibility obvious. The sampled MensXP primary guide did not present a shared price/size/check-date table. | An inclusive ₹1,000 guide with scent-family/format/size filters and verified eligible variants. Explain whether offers, samples and delivery charges affect the stated budget. |
| **K04 body lotion** | Nykaa supplies filters, an editorial guide and a separate footer price table dated 8 October 2026. Its collection also displays products beyond adult dry-skin body lotion. | Curate tightly for the stated adult body-care task and put relevant distinctions near the shortlist. **Dated prices are not absent from Nykaa**; Glowwise's improvement is consistent contextual presentation. | One guide with verified adult body-lotion options; distinguish face creams, baby products and body products instead of treating them as interchangeable. Show texture/fragrance fields and comparable unit cost where valid. |
| **K05 sensitive-skin cleanser** | Strong publisher/brand coverage and a Smytten dermatologist-recommended title appear in the observed set. | Answer the selection task using evidence and stated limits rather than adopting an expert-endorsement title. A sitewide missing-topic claim is not established. | Conditional guide with attributed product information, research method and links to appropriate sources. No invented expert review or personal results. |
| **K06 beard oil** | Gillette and Smytten already discuss benefits; Healthline and a community result also appear. | Explain the distinction between cosmetic conditioning, marketing claims and demonstrated outcomes. The useful opportunity is evidence quality and clarity, not “nobody covers benefits.” | A concise sourced explainer, relevant oil-versus-balm context and optional product links. Verify claims before publication. |
| **K07 trimmers** | Retailer catalogs, videos, Pricee and Smartprix already support price/specification discovery. | Provide a manageable comparison tied to the exact ₹1,500 cap, with specification sources and maintenance/warranty context. This is a hypothesis for a better decision flow, not an audited absence at those sites. | Working comparison for up to three products: settings, runtime, charging, cleaning, attachments, warranty and checked price. Label missing specifications and avoid fictional testing. |
| **Across guides** | Competitors already combine commerce and information in several ways. | Apply the same evidence and interaction standard across all six grooming categories, reducing the work required to compare options. | Shared content/product fields, readable mobile comparisons, save/compare controls and clearly labelled retailer destinations. |

**Commercial transparency:** Glowwise should distinguish editorial research, manufacturer statements, actual testing and affiliate/sponsored relationships. These distinctions must remain visible on mobile. No “independent,” “tested” or “dermatologist-approved” claim is justified merely by researching a competitor.

### Requirements carried into implementation

- **Product data:** Explicit variant, pack size/unit, category, source URL, checked INR price/date and verified comparison attributes. Missing data stays labelled.
- **Guide template:** Quick answer, selection method, useful contents links, comparison/shortlist, limitations, relevant FAQs, sources, author and update date.
- **Working actions:** Search/filter, compare up to three, local save and retailer handoff, consistent with the Source of Truth. No on-site checkout or invented live inventory.
- **Mobile and desktop:** Preserve the same sources, dates, product fields and actions. Use readable stacked comparisons or accessible horizontal tables; no essential hover-only information.
- **Trust and footer:** Carry forward About, Contact, editorial/research policy, privacy, terms and applicable commercial disclosures. Publish policies that match actual functionality rather than borrowing a retailer's returns/shipping promises.
- **Later measurement:** After deployment and Search Console/GA4 setup, review actual query impressions, clicks and relevant product/retailer actions. Revisit the SERPs and priorities from observed performance. No ranking or traffic outcome is guaranteed by this research.
