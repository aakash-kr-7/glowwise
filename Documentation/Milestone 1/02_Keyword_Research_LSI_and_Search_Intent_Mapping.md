## 1. Requirement and research method

The supplied handbook requires 10–15 primary/focus and secondary keywords with search volume, keyword difficulty and classified intent, preferably KD below 20 for primary targets. It also requires a structured table of 5–10 long-tail and “LSI” terms with competition and relevance scores.

Research followed Glowwise’s approved positioning: affordable grooming discovery for Indian adults approximately 18–30, across genders, with six categories and equally complete mobile and desktop experiences.

1. Started with product-type seeds and expanded toward needs, questions, comparisons and budgets.
2. Used **Ubersuggest** for seed suggestions, a perfume matching report, a body-lotion overview and Google autocomplete expansion.
3. Used **Keyword Tool: Free SEO Ideas** for seven Google suggestion sets with India and English explicitly selected. Available cached metrics were retained; text-only suggestions were not assigned invented metrics.
4. Read **Semrush Keyword Overview** and its visible variation/question tables for nine seeds through Browser. Preserved screenshots and report text. These provide the consistent baseline below.
5. Inspected a live shampoo search to check an ambiguous intent classification. This was an intent spot-check, not the full competitor/SERP criterion.
6. Consulted **Ahrefs** documentation and its LSI explanation. Its connector’s plan restriction prevented keyword metrics; no Ahrefs volume or difficulty values are claimed.

The [Evidence Index](<../References/Milestone 1/02 Keyword Research Intent Mapping/Evidence_Index.md>) maps sources, screenshots, saved tool responses and access limitations. Original course materials and the earlier unfinished draft were preserved; historical draft metrics were not copied into these tables.

## 2. How to read the data

| Field | Meaning and interpretation |
|---|---|
| **Estimated monthly volume** | Semrush’s national monthly average, based on its preceding 12-month data window. Displayed values such as 6.6K are expanded to approximately 6,600. Searches are not unique people, visits or projected Glowwise traffic. |
| **KD, 0–100** | Semrush’s organic keyword difficulty estimate. Lower values indicate comparatively lower estimated difficulty; KD 18 does not mean an 18% probability of ranking. This is standard KD, not a domain-specific Personal KD score. |
| **Intent** | Glowwise’s editorial classification of the visitor’s task: informational (I), commercial investigation (C), transactional (T) or navigational (N). Mixed intent is retained where useful. |
| **Primary/focus** | The main query direction assigned to a proposed page. A focus candidate is not a ranking guarantee or evidence that its page exists. |
| **Secondary** | A closely connected phrase, supporting question or broader topic that a relevant page can also address. It does not automatically require another page. |
| **Unknown / n/a** | Missing provider data. It is not zero demand or zero competition. |

Semrush reports displayed **India, Desktop, 8 October 2026**. The Desktop label records the report context; Semrush explains that its volume figures include **mobile and desktop** searches. English describes the phrases researched, not an additional language filter in Semrush’s national database. Ubersuggest used the resolved India location **2356** with `en`; Keyword Tool returned India/English metadata. [Semrush volume methodology](https://www.semrush.com/kb/683-what-is-search-volume-in-semrush), [Semrush KD definition](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1158-what-is-kd).

### Why the focus candidates were selected

- **K01 — 6,600 searches, KD 18:** A meaningful need-based haircare topic below the preferred threshold. Combine practical explanation with researched product choices. The lower-KD K10 question can be answered on the same page.
- **K02 — 1,600 searches, KD 18:** A narrower measured alternative to the 60,500-volume sunscreen phrase at KD 22. Use a natural page title and explain preferences and budget options without promising universal suitability.
- **K03 — 1,300 searches, KD 19:** Directly expresses affordability. Related men’s and women’s queries can inform inclusive sections and filters within a shared collection or guide.
- **K04 — 140 searches, KD 13:** Smaller demand with a clear purchase question. It provides an initial focus for a broader body-lotion guide without treating the 18,100-volume parent phrase as an easy first target.
- **K05 — 390 searches, KD 20:** Exactly 20, so it does **not** satisfy a strict “below 20” preference. Retain conditionally because the need fits Glowwise; inspect competitors before prioritising it.
- **K06 — 590 searches, KD 24:** Supports an explanatory beard-care page and six-category coverage. It requires evidence for benefits and limitations; it must not become a beard-growth promise.
- **K07 — 720 searches, KD 29:** Strong budget fit for a working tool comparison, but above the preferred threshold. Treat it as a planned product/content topic requiring stronger competition review, not a quick-win claim.

The eight secondary terms broaden the answers on these pages. Their larger volumes do not justify separate near-duplicate articles or a summed traffic forecast.

## 4. Long-tail, related terms and semantic coverage — 10 terms

The handbook uses **“LSI.”** Here, that label means useful related phrases and topic vocabulary. No mathematical LSI analysis, Google “LSI score” or ranking bonus is claimed. Ahrefs distinguishes the literal technology from the related terms commonly meant in SEO practice. Google also advises writing naturally rather than forcing every query variation into a page. [Ahrefs explanation](https://ahrefs.com/blog/lsi-keywords/), [Google SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide).

These are specific multi-word variants; specificity alone does not establish low search demand or low competition.

### Relevance scoring method

**Editorial relevance, 0–5 = category fit (0–2) + usefulness to a visitor’s decision (0–2) + explicit budget or additional specification (0–1).** Direct category fit and a clear decision receive two points each. An explicit price cap or extra specification such as SPF 50 receives one further point. These are transparent project judgments, not provider-generated metrics.

**Competition score below = the exact phrase’s measured Semrush KD, 0–100.** Semantic vocabulary in the final column is proposed content coverage; it does not inherit the query’s KD or receive a fabricated search volume.

## 5. Search intent and page mapping

### Intent decisions

| Intent | Actual or illustrative query | Glowwise response |
|---|---|---|
| **Informational** | **Measured:** “beard oil benefits” | Explain purpose, evidence, limitations and use context; connect readers to relevant product information. |
| **Commercial investigation** | **Measured:** “best trimmer under 1500” | Compare eligible products, verified specifications, checked INR prices and trade-offs. |
| **Transactional, with commercial overlap** | **Measured:** “cetaphil face wash for sensitive skin” | Help visitors identify verified brand options and reach the relevant product/retailer destination. This is not an on-site checkout promise. |
| **Navigational** | **Illustrative future query:** “Glowwise product finder” | Direct access to Glowwise’s finder. No existing search volume, KD or branded demand is claimed. |

Questions beginning with “which” can express commercial investigation. Conversely, an explanatory “benefits” query can remain informational even when products are discussed.

**Ambiguous shampoo intent:** Semrush labels K01 informational. The live India-parameter search showed retailer results, product listings, recommendation videos, questions and a guide result. This supports **commercial investigation with informational support** as Glowwise’s working classification. E15–E16 preserve the observed formats. The phrase is not relabelled merely to inflate commercial value; the page must satisfy both learning and selection. One search context is not a representative ranking study.

**Perfume intent:** Semrush displays informational and commercial labels for K03. Preserve that overlap: explain scent formats and selection criteria alongside eligible products.

### Proposed ownership of pages

Paths below are **proposed**, relative to the future domain. They are not implemented URLs or a substitute for the later complete site hierarchy.

| Proposed page | Focus keyword | Secondary/supporting coverage | Main action |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/guides/shampoo-for-dry-frizzy-hair/` | K01 | K10–K11; L01–L02, L10 | Learn, filter relevant products, compare |
| `/guides/sunscreen-for-oily-skin/` | K02 | K13; L05–L06; budget section | Understand differences and shortlist |
| `/guides/perfumes-under-1000/` | K03 | L07–L08; men’s, women’s and relevant unisex options where verified | Filter eligible choices and save |
| `/guides/body-lotion-for-dry-skin/` | K04 | K08–K09; L03 | Compare textures, attributes and prices |
| `/guides/face-wash-for-sensitive-skin/` | K05, conditional | K12; L04; verified brand options for K15 | Identify options, then inspect products |
| `/guides/beard-oil-benefits/` | K06, conditional | Oil-versus-balm explanation where useful | Understand purpose and limitations |
| `/guides/trimmers-under-1500/` | K07, conditional | K14; L09 | Compare specifications and retailer options |

**Cannibalisation controls:** Give each selection need one guide owner. Product pages provide distinct factual product detail and retailer links; category pages organise broader discovery. Filters and changed word order do not automatically produce new indexable landing pages. A separate collection or brand page should be introduced only when its purpose and content are sufficiently distinct. The later architecture criterion will finalise canonical/indexing rules and the full taxonomy.

**Mobile and desktop:** The same guidance, sources, price dates, filtering, comparison and saving actions must remain available on both. Layout and inputs adapt to the device; essential information must not depend on hover or disappear from the mobile version.

Sources: R05, R06, R09; Ubersuggest saved perfume matching and body-lotion overview responses; Keyword Tool saved perfume, body-lotion and sensitive-skin responses. E03 also confirms Ubersuggest’s perfume overview at 2.4K and SD 21.

**No averaging or substitution:** Different databases, update schedules and estimation methods can produce different values. This research does not establish which estimate is closest to actual Google demand. Keyword Tool’s advertiser competition and top-of-page bid are paid-search measures; they are **not organic KD**, and the bid range is not CPC. [Keyword Tool metric definitions](https://keywordtool.io/search-volume/google).

**Freshness:** Retrieval date is not necessarily the date every metric was recalculated. Ubersuggest’s body-lotion response included a monthly series ending in October 2025; its perfume interface displayed difficulty from about three months earlier. Those limitations are retained rather than presenting the figures as newly recalculated October 2026 demand. No paid refresh was requested.

**Coverage:** Semrush’s nine seed reports and their visible suggestions are a focused research pass, not an exhaustive export of every niche keyword. Autocomplete can return irrelevant, non-Indian or unsupported-claim variants even with India selected; these require screening. Ahrefs supplied no keyword metrics in this pass.

## 7. Terms to hold or exclude

| Term or pattern | Decision and reason |
|---|---|
| “sunscreen for oily skin under 500” | Hold as a useful budget section/topic. R04 returned no volume or KD. Do not present it as a measured low-competition keyword or assign the parent query’s metrics to it. |
| “beard oil vs balm” | Useful explanatory support. R02 reports India volume 10 and KD unavailable; low volume does not prove easy ranking. |
| “dermatologist recommended…” | Exclude as a focus promise without authentic, attributable support. R01 exposes a KD 11 shampoo variant, but the score does not justify an unsupported endorsement. |
| “without chemicals,” whitening, guaranteed growth or guaranteed results | Do not adopt misleading or unsupported promises from suggestion lists. Assess any legitimate topic separately against the editorial policy. |
| “audio trimmer,” “online mp3 trimmer,” video-trimming terms | Exclude: software/editing intent is outside grooming tools, despite matching the word “trimmer.” |
| Pakistan/Bangladesh-specific price queries or unrelated luxury-brand variants | Exclude from this India affordability shortlist unless a future researched page has a clear, relevant purpose. |
| Separate pages for every “best,” “which” or reordered variant | Exclude: answer shared intent on a substantial owner page. |

## 8. Decisions carried forward

- **Initial SEO focus:** Four lower-difficulty candidates: K01–K04. Their pages still need competitor review, original content and verified product data.
- **Conditional coverage:** K05–K07 support the complete grooming product, with KD 20/24/29 recorded honestly. These are deliberate exceptions to the preferred below-20 threshold.
- **Semantic approach:** Explain useful concepts naturally. Relevance scores describe Glowwise’s editorial fit; no proprietary Google relevance score is implied.
- **Research boundary:** This file establishes the keyword set and intent mapping. It does not claim measured rankings, a full competitor gap, traffic forecasts or implemented pages.
- **Later measurement:** Once the website is deployed and analytics are configured, review actual query impressions, clicks, page engagement and retailer actions. Revise targets from observed performance rather than assuming search-volume estimates will become traffic.
