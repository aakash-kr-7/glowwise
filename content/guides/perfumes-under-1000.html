The decision this guide helps you make

At this budget, this is a deliberately narrow comparison: six SKINN eau de parfum families in 20 ml packs. It is not a claim to have tested every perfume sold in India. A small bottle lowers the purchase commitment, while the published note pyramid helps identify a direction you would like to investigate.

How we selected and ordered the recommendations

Raw opens the edit as a fresh direction, followed by Celeste’s fruity floral, Sheer’s softer floral and Nude’s rose-led direction. Steele is the warmer spicy alternative and could be first if that is your preference. Verge is last because its current 20 ml page says Coming Back Soon. This preference-led order is not a projection, wear-time or universal “best perfume” ranking.

Practical buying guidance

Read a note pyramid as a description

Top, heart and base notes describe the brand’s intended progression. They are not a complete ingredient list and do not establish how many hours a fragrance lasts. We have not worn these perfumes, compared their projection or tested them in heat. Use the individual product page for the current pyramid; collection copy can be abbreviated or inconsistent.

The budget applies to this exact size

The prices here belong to 20 ml bottles. A 50 ml or 100 ml selector on the same manufacturer page is a different purchase. Conditional coupons, delivery and bundles are excluded. Raw and Steele are ₹596 at this check; Celeste, Sheer and Nude show ₹556. Verge’s ₹556 is a collection reference while its current direct page has no active purchase price.

Try before buying when possible

A note that sounds attractive may feel different on a blotter or your skin. If a shop offers a genuine sample, use that opportunity without interpreting a brief first impression as an all-day test. You can also compare the smallest available pack and the retailer’s return policy. An eau de parfum label does not supply an exact concentration percentage or guaranteed longevity.
