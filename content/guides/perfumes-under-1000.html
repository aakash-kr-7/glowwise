A budget is only useful when the bottle is identified

A perfume name can cover several sizes, concentrations or gift sets. For this guide, “under ₹1,000” means the identified single item was strictly below ₹1,000 when checked on 9 October 2026. It excludes delivery and any conditional coupon. A price for 20 ml does not put the 50 ml bottle in the same budget.

Our starting edit uses six distinct SKINN eau de parfum fragrance families in their 20 ml variants. They are genuine official-store destinations, rather than unverified marketplace sellers or decants. The narrow brand coverage is a research boundary, not a claim that other brands are inferior. We have not worn these perfumes or measured longevity.

Use scent notes as directions, not a verdict

For Raw’s identified variant, the manufacturer describes an opening of bergamot, watery fruits and mandarin. The official category material describes grapefruit and ginger for Celeste, pear and strawberry for Sheer, and lychee and raspberry for Nude. Those descriptions are useful vocabulary for investigating a preference, not firsthand impressions written by Glowwise.

We leave unverified note details unknown for other records. A note list also does not establish how long the scent lasts, how far it projects, how it behaves on your skin or whether it feels appropriate in a shared workplace. “Fresh”, “warm” and “sweet” are starting questions to explore through a sample or store visit when possible.

Think about the real first purchase

A small bottle lowers the total item cost and takes less storage space, but may have a different cost per millilitre from a larger bottle. You need not maximize quantity when you are unsure whether you like the fragrance. Compare the actual 20 ml variant, delivery charges and retailer return terms before buying.

The manufacturer markets some fragrances for men and others for women. You can use those labels to find the correct source product, while choosing a scent by your own preferences. Our catalog does not restrict saving, finding or comparing based on gender.

What the guide cannot tell you

We do not have a tested “longest lasting” winner, an invented review score or a claim that these are the best perfumes in India. We also cannot authenticate a different seller’s stock through an official-store price. Follow the genuine destination, check the current variant and make your own decision about scent tolerance.
