Practical buying guidance

Washable blades are not a waterproof body

The S2 and S3 manufacturer pages state IPX7. The other models’ washable-blade wording does not establish that you can rinse the entire device or immerse it. Read the actual manual and disconnect charging before cleaning as instructed. A cleaning shortcut is not worth relying on a rating belonging to another model.

Runtime and charging answer different questions

Runtime is the manufacturer’s stated operating duration, not how fast a device recharges. Power P1 and SmartOne S3 state 160-minute runtime and 90-minute charging. TurboLite states 90 minutes for each. Power Lite and TurboOne have a verified 90-minute runtime here, but we leave charging time unknown rather than copying a figure from a related model. Actual results vary with use and battery condition.

Choose controls you will actually use

S3’s three speed settings, travel lock and memory positioning may matter if you travel or share a routine across lengths. Its “AI memory” wording is a brand feature name, not evidence of automated grooming advice. If you only need a basic trim, the lower-priced Power Lite may fit your purchase better. Check the supplied combs and length range on the exact model’s current page before ordering.
