Compare a model, not a generic trimmer promise

A budget beard trimmer is easier to assess when the model number, charging connection and cleaning instructions are visible. For this guide, “under ₹1,500” means the identified model’s listed item price was strictly below ₹1,500 on 9 October 2026. Delivery and conditional coupons are excluded.

Three differences worth your attention

Charging connection: the Power Lite and Power Series P1 pages specify Micro USB. The SmartOne S3 specifies Type-C USB. Verify the included cable and adapter requirements; a familiar connection can be a more practical preference than a marketing feature name.

Cleaning: a washable blade is not the same claim as a waterproof device. The S3 page lists IPX7; several other pages list washable blades without establishing the same whole-device rating. Follow the exact model’s instructions, disconnect power when required and do not generalize one model’s cleaning method to another.

Charge time and runtime: the P1 and S3 pages list 90-minute charging and 160-minute operation figures. Those are manufacturer claims, not a laboratory comparison by Glowwise. A maximum runtime number does not establish cutting comfort, battery life after prolonged ownership or a better personal result.

Read the length settings carefully

The selected group lists 40 settings over a 0.5–20 mm range. Check the model page and comb configuration before assuming that every setting works without changing attachments. A precise model number is a better basis for a manual lookup than a retailer’s broad product-family name.

Start with the guard and instructions appropriate to your task. The retailer’s branding around “smart” or “AI” features does not mean Glowwise’s finder is AI, and we do not convert that branding into an independent quality rating.

Choose a practical shortlist

  1. Pick only models currently available to your delivery location, unless you are deliberately keeping an unavailable model as a reference.
  2. Select up to three beard trimmers and inspect charging, cleaning, known charge time and the identified model.
  3. Read warranty and return conditions on the current retailer page, including any registration or extension conditions.
  4. Keep total item cost separate from delivery and additional charging accessories.
